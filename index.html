Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with my insurance company?

Yes, we work with all major insurance providers. We can communicate directly with them to handle the appraisal and billing process, making it hassle-free for you.

Do I need an appointment for an estimate?

While walk-ins are welcome, we recommend calling ahead at (508) 756-0842 to ensure someone is available to inspect your vehicle immediately.

How long will the repair take?

Repair time depends on the severity of the damage and parts availability. Once we inspect your vehicle, we will provide you with a realistic estimated completion date.

Do you offer towing?

Yes, if your car is not safe to drive, give us a call and we can help arrange towing to our shop.
